Our story

Two homes. One wardrobe.

Beldi Collective was born between Morocco and Europe. We grew up with things that were made properly: bread from the neighbourhood oven, a djellaba cut by the tailor down the street. That's what beldi means to us, and it's how we want to make clothes.
