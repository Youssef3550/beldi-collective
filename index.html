Our story

Two homes. One wardrobe.

Beldi Collective was created with a simple goal: to bring clean, heavyweight streetwear into your daily rotation. Drawing inspiration from North African heritage without falling into loud clichés, the brand translates the meaning of beldi (authentic, traditional, homegrown) into modern, everyday silhouettes.

Small batches

Every piece is made to order, so nothing is overproduced.

Made to last

Heavyweight fabrics and embroidery that survives the wash.

Two cultures

Moroccan roots, Belgian streets, worn everywhere.
