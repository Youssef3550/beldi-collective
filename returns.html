Help

Returns

You have days to change your mind. Here is how it works.

Made to order, so check your size first

Because every piece is custom-produced to order, return shipping costs are covered by the buyer, and we recommend checking our exact size charts before ordering.

Your 14-day right of withdrawal

You can withdraw from your purchase within days after you (or someone you name) receive the order, without giving a reason. Tell us by email at hello@example.com with your order number and the pieces you want to return. You then have another 14 days to send them back.

  • Pieces must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition.
  • Return shipping is paid by you. We recommend a tracked service; we can't refund parcels that get lost on the way to us.
  • We refund the price of the returned pieces and the original standard shipping cost within 14 days after we receive your withdrawal, once the pieces are back with us (or you show proof of sending), using the same payment method you used.

Misprints, damage or the wrong item

If a piece arrives with a print or embroidery error, a defect, damage, or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo within 30 days of delivery. We replace it or refund it at no cost to you, return shipping included. Your legal guarantee of conformity (two years in the EU) always applies.

Exchanges

Every piece is made for one order, so we don't do direct swaps. Return the piece under the right of withdrawal and place a new order in the size you need.

Demo policy, not legal advice. Have it checked before you start selling.
