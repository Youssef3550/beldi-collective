Shipping

Every piece is made when you order it. Delivery time is production plus transit.

1. Production: 2–5 business days

Your piece is printed or embroidered to order at our production partner's European facilities. This usually takes 2–5 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays). Busy periods such as a drop or the weeks before Christmas can add a few days.

2. Transit: depends on where you live

DestinationCarrier transitTotal, order to door
Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg2–4 business days4–9 business days
France, Germany3–5 business days5–10 business days
Rest of the EU3–7 business days5–12 business days
United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway4–8 business days6–13 business days

Transit times are carrier estimates and not guaranteed. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves production. If you order several pieces, they may arrive in separate parcels.

Shipping costs

Standard shipping is per order. Orders over ship free. For the UK, Switzerland and Norway, import duties and taxes may be charged by your country on delivery.

Something wrong?

Parcel late, lost or damaged? Email hello@example.com with your order number. See Returns for misprints and faulty items.
